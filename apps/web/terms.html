TERMS OF SERVICE

Clear rules for safer testing.

These terms describe the basic rules for using AgentGuard to test, monitor, and protect AI agents.

Last updated October 25, 2026 · 4 min read

1. The service

AgentGuard provides AI agent security testing, evidence, runtime protection, workspace administration, and related support. Features, limits, and evidence retention depend on the plan shown on the Pricing page and in the authenticated dashboard.

2. Your account and workspace

You are responsible for the accuracy of account information, protecting credentials, selecting workspace members, and all activity performed through your workspace. Keep access keys current and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

3. Safe testing requirement

You must own or be authorized to test every endpoint connected to AgentGuard. Use a staging or mock executor whenever possible. Do not submit secrets, payment credentials, or real customer data to the public Attack Lab. You remain responsible for the behavior of your own agent, tools, integrations, and downstream systems.

4. Acceptable use

You may not use AgentGuard to attack systems without permission, bypass access controls, process unlawful content, interfere with the service, evade plan limits, or expose another person’s data. We may suspend access when necessary to protect users, the service, or the public.

5. Billing and payments

Paid plans are priced in USD and renew manually unless the checkout or an order confirmation says otherwise. Direct crypto checkout accepts the exact USDT amount shown for the order on the TRC20 network. You are responsible for using the correct network, amount, and wallet; blockchain transfers may be irreversible and network fees are paid by the sender. Access is activated only after on-chain verification.

6. Evidence and intellectual property

You retain rights to your agent, prompts, responses, and workspace content. You grant AgentGuard the limited permission needed to process that content to provide the service, generate evidence, secure the platform, and support your workspace. AgentGuard retains its software, scenarios, methods, and product materials.

7. Availability and liability

Security testing reduces risk but cannot guarantee that an agent, integration, or third party will never be compromised. The service is provided on an as-available basis. To the extent permitted by law, AgentGuard is not liable for indirect losses or side effects caused by an endpoint, tool, integration, or customer configuration.

8. Contact

Questions about these terms, billing, or a security issue can be sent to hello@agentguard.dev. Please have qualified counsel review these terms for the jurisdictions and commercial model in which you launch.
